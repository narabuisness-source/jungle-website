FACTS ABOUT THE JUNGLE

>

01 BIODIVERSITY

Tropical rainforests are among Earth's most biologically diverse ecosystems, containing an enormous variety of plants, fungi, and animals.

02 THE CANOPY

The upper layer of a rainforest forms a dense canopy that can block much of the sunlight from reaching the forest floor.

03 RAINFALL

Some tropical rainforests receive more than 2,000 mm of rain per year, creating their characteristic humid environment.

04 THE AMAZON

The Amazon rainforest covers roughly 5.5 million km² and stretches across several South American countries.

05 FOREST LAYERS

Rainforests are commonly divided into four layers: emergent, canopy, understory, and forest floor.

06 WATER CYCLE

Trees return huge amounts of water vapor to the atmosphere through transpiration, helping drive regional rainfall.

07 ANCIENT ECOSYSTEMS

Tropical rainforests have existed in various forms for tens of millions of years, making them some of Earth's ancient ecosystems.

08 NEW DISCOVERIES

Scientists continue to identify new species in tropical forests, meaning much of their biodiversity remains incompletely documented.
